# Reading Notes

This is my reading log. For each book I read, I type out its **table of contents** and, where it's useful, its **bibliography** — then add my own notes, reactions, and the passages worth keeping.

Typing a book's structure by hand is slow on purpose. It's how I read closely: the table of contents shows how the author *built* the argument, and the bibliography shows the conversation the book is part of. Over time these notes link to each other, so a citation in one book becomes a path to the next.

## How these notes are organized

Each book lives in [`books/`](books/) with the same shape:

1. **Frontmatter** — title, author, year, ISBN, the dates I read it, a rating, and tags.
2. **Table of contents** — typed out, chapter by chapter.
3. **Notes & reactions** — what I'm thinking as I read.
4. **Quotes** — passages worth keeping.
5. **Bibliography / sources** — works the book draws on, linked to other notes where I have them.
