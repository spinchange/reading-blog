## Notes & Reactions

- "Normal science" is the day-to-day puzzle-solving that happens *inside* an accepted paradigm — and most science is this, not revolution.
- A paradigm doesn't fall because it's disproven; it falls when a *better* one is available to replace it. Anomalies pile up first.
- The "invisibility of revolutions" chapter is the sneaky one: textbooks get rewritten after a shift, so the discontinuity disappears from the record.

## Quotes

> The transfer of allegiance from paradigm to paradigm is a conversion experience that cannot be forced.

> Normal science does not aim at novelties of fact or theory and, when successful, finds none.
